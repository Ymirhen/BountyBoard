# Black Market Bounty Board

Black Market bounties: one-shot contracts hosted by the guild's GMs.

**Board:** https://ymirhen.github.io/BountyBoard/

## Poster art prompt (ChatGPT)

Paste this into ChatGPT to generate a sumi-e style wanted poster for a bounty. Copy the style block exactly as it is, and change only the **Subject** line for each bounty. The text areas are left empty on purpose, because ChatGPT garbles lettering. The board shows the title and reward itself.

```
Japanese wanted poster ("tehaisho") painted in black sumi-e ink on aged cream washi paper. Loose expressive brush strokes, dry-brush texture, ragged torn edges on the figure, heavy ink splatter and flecks around it. Ink: black ink only. A few brush strokes may thin out into gray where the brush runs dry, but only in small amounts; everything else is solid black, with the bare paper left as negative space for highlights. No ink washes or gray fills. Composition: a single dark figure in the center, one huge bold calligraphy title area at the top right (leave it empty), two vertical columns of small calligraphy at the left (leave them empty), and three small square hanko seals at the bottom right. Seals: stamped in vermilion red ink, each one worn differently and at random: uneven ink coverage, faded or missing patches, chipped corners, broken outlines, slight smudges, as if pressed by hand with old stamps. The seals are the only color; no other colors, no gradients, high contrast, vertical portrait layout.
Subject: [WHO: race/build/outfit], [HEADGEAR/SILHOUETTE DETAIL], holding [WEAPON], [POSE/MOOD].
```

Subject examples:

- `Subject: a hooded blood elf assassin in a long tattered coat, hood drawn low, holding two curved daggers, crouched and ready to strike.`
- `Subject: a hulking orc brute in layered fur and scrap armor, wearing a horned helm, holding a huge cleaver, standing heavy and still.`
- `Subject: a goblin alchemist with oversized goggles and a bandolier of flasks, holding a smoking vial, grinning mid-step.`

Tips:

- Keep each subject to one figure with one or two defining details. Fewer details keep the ink style dominant.
- To make another poster in the same style, ask in the same chat: "Match the style of the previous image exactly, new subject: …"
- In the bounty builder, press **Upload image** next to "Poster art URL" and pick the file. It goes into the bounty's Drive folder and the link is filled in for you.

## Unit card portrait prompt (ChatGPT)

Paste this into ChatGPT to generate a portrait for an NPC or enemy unit card, in the same ink style as the posters. Copy the style block exactly as it is, and change only the **Subject** line. The card crops the image to roughly 4:5 from the top, so the head and shoulders are kept in the upper part of the frame.

```
Character portrait for a game unit card, painted in black sumi-e ink on aged cream washi paper, matching a Japanese wanted poster ("tehaisho") style. Loose expressive brush strokes, dry-brush texture, ragged torn edges where the figure fades out, light ink splatter and flecks. Ink: black ink only. A few brush strokes may thin out into gray where the brush runs dry, but only in small amounts; everything else is solid black, with the bare paper left as negative space for highlights; no color, no red seals, no text, no calligraphy, no border or frame. Composition: one figure shown from the chest up, slightly turned, face and headgear in the upper half of the image, eyes about one third down from the top, the figure filling most of the width, plain paper background. High contrast, crisp facial features, vertical portrait layout (2:3).
Subject: [WHO: race/build/outfit], [FACE/HEADGEAR DETAIL], [EXPRESSION/MOOD], [ONE PROP visible at the shoulder or chest, optional].
```

Subject examples:

- `Subject: a young pandaren monk in a simple wrapped gi, a straw kasa hat pushed back, calm and watchful, a wooden staff resting on her shoulder.`
- `Subject: a scarred human pirate captain in a long coat, tricorn hat and eyepatch, sneering, a cutlass hilt at his chest.`
- `Subject: a giant porcupine beast with quills bristling outward, small fierce eyes, snarling.`

Tips:

- Enemies read best with a hard expression and a strong silhouette; friendly NPCs with a softer turn of the head.
- To keep a set consistent, ask in the same chat: "Match the style of the previous portrait exactly, new subject: …"
- In the bounty builder, press **Upload image** next to the unit's "Portrait image URL" field and pick the file.
